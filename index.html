Facilities at Tirth Group Tuition

Classrooms :

All class rooms are equipped with whiteboards, comfortable seating, good lighting & proper ventilation for focused learning.

Smart Classrooms :

Use of smart screens and digital tools for teaching. Audio-Visual enabled classrooms for better concept visualization.

Materials & Study Content :

Study material based on NCERT/GSEB, prepared by experienced teachers, in both Gujarati & English medium; assignments, test papers, and weekly worksheets.

Library Support :

Access to reference books and question banks for extra practice.

Doubt / Difficulty Sessions :

Students can ask doubts before/after class or during the week; extra support for topics where students struggle.

Academic Calendar & Exams :

Year-long academic calendar planned in advance; weekly & monthly tests; exam preparation for board & school exams.

Parent Communication :

Regular updates to parents regarding student progress, attendance, and test scores via SMS or meetings.

Transportation :

Van/Rickshaw facility coordination available for students (optional).

SINCE 2001

Trusted Coaching Institute
In Gandhinagar

Tirth Group Tuition was established with a simple yet powerful vision - to provide quality education that transforms lives. With over 24 years of experience, we have grown from a small coaching center to a trusted name in education.

We cover all core subjects - Maths, Science, English, and Social Science for Class 1 to 10. Our commitment to excellence and personalized attention has made us the preferred choice for parents who want the best for their children.

  • Expert tuition for CBSE & GSEB boards
  • Personalized academic guidance
  • Regular tests & performance tracking
